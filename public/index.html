02 / OUR STORY

Born from the
love of a good table.

Ember & Spice began with a simple idea: take the flavours we grew up with and give them room to evolve.

Our kitchen celebrates regional Indian produce through live-fire cooking, house-made ferments and ingredients sourced from people we trust. The result is familiar, surprising and designed to be shared.
